Campus Life

World-Class Facilities

Every facility designed to maximise learning, comfort and creativity

Central Library

5-floor digital library with 2 lakh+ books, 50,000+ e-journals, and a silent study zone open 24/7.

  • RFID-automated book management
  • International journal access (IEEE, Elsevier)
  • Reading rooms & group discussion pods
  • Digital archives going back to 1985

Research Laboratories

40+ state-of-the-art labs equipped with the latest instruments for engineering, science and medical research.

  • AI & Robotics Lab
  • Genomics & Molecular Biology Lab
  • Material Science & Nanotechnology Lab
  • Climate & Environmental Research Centre

Hostel Facilities

Separate boys' and girls' hostels with modern amenities in a safe, well-maintained environment.

  • AC & Non-AC room options
  • 24/7 security & CCTV surveillance
  • In-house mess with 4 meal timings
  • Gym, recreation room & laundry
  • High-speed Wi-Fi in every room

Sports Complex

Olympic-standard sports infrastructure across 50 acres of dedicated sports grounds.

  • Olympic-size swimming pool
  • Indoor stadium (basketball, badminton)
  • Cricket ground & football field
  • Tennis, squash & volleyball courts

Health Centre

On-campus health facilities with doctors on duty around the clock for students and staff.

  • 24/7 emergency care
  • Specialist doctors 5 days a week
  • Mental health counselling
  • Dental & eye care clinic

Innovation Hub

Startup incubation centre supporting student entrepreneurs from ideation to funding.

  • Co-working space for 200 students
  • Investor networking events
  • 20+ startups incubated annually
  • Seed funding up to ₹10 Lakhs
