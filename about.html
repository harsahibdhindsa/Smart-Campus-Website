Our Story

A Legacy of Excellence

Takshashila University was founded in 1985 with a vision to democratise quality education in India. What began as a single-campus engineering college in Sector 14 has today blossomed into a multi-campus institution with 18,000+ students, 1,200+ faculty members and 80+ academic programs.

Over four decades, we have produced 1.2 lakh alumni who now hold leadership positions across government, industry and academia — from CEOs of Fortune 500 companies to Padma Bhushan awardees.

We are NAAC A++ accredited, ranked #3 in NIRF national rankings, and have active research collaborations with MIT, NUS Singapore, and Oxford.
